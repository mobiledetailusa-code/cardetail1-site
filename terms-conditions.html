Booking & Service Terms

  • Booking request: submitting the booking form does not guarantee the appointment until Cardetail1 confirms by text, call, or email.
  • Time windows: arrival windows are estimates only. Traffic, weather, prior jobs, parking, tolls, and access issues may cause delays.
  • Weather: rain, storms, freezing temperatures, unsafe heat, wind, or lack of safe covered access may require rescheduling with no penalty.
  • Cancellation / rescheduling: please request changes at least 24 hours before the appointment. Rescheduling is preferred over cancellation.
  • Late cancellation: cancellations made within 24 hours, same-day cancellation, unavailable vehicle/vessel, no-show, denied access, or unsafe location may result in a cancellation fee or loss of deposit.
  • Fairness policy: if weather, emergency, or reasonable schedule conflict occurs, Cardetail1 may offer rescheduling to avoid unfair charges.
  • Final price: online totals are starting estimates. Excessive dirt, pet hair, odor, stains, mold, oxidation, black streaks, distance, tolls, parking, difficult access, or add-ons may change the final price.
  • Unsafe / out-of-scope conditions: biohazard, pest contamination, abandoned units, sewage, mold, severe odor, unsafe roofs, or damaged surfaces may require an estimate or may be declined.

Payment & Deposit

  • Deposit: a refundable deposit (25% of the estimated total) may be authorized or charged to secure your appointment slot.
  • Secure processing: all card payments are handled directly by Stripe. Cardetail1 does not see or store raw card numbers.
  • Authorization vs. capture: for vessels and units priced by length, the deposit may be authorized as a temporary hold and the final amount captured after service.
  • Refunds: deposits are refundable when you cancel or reschedule within the terms above. Late cancellations, no-shows, denied access, or unsafe conditions may forfeit the deposit per the cancellation policy.
  • Final payment: the remaining balance is collected after confirmation or on completion of service unless booking terms state otherwise.
