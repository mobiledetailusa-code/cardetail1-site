1. Booking Request & Confirmation

  • Request only: Submitting a booking request does not guarantee an appointment. Cardetail1 reviews location, vehicle condition, service type, weather, access, and availability before confirming.
  • Confirmation: Your appointment is confirmed only when you receive a separate confirmation from Cardetail1. Until then, your request is under review.
  • Time windows: Arrival windows are estimates. Traffic, weather, prior jobs, parking, and access issues may cause delays.
  • Right to decline: Cardetail1 reserves the right to decline or reschedule a booking based on location safety, vehicle condition, hazardous materials, or operational limitations.

2. Payment Details & Optional Saved Cards

  • No payment at request: No card or payment method is required to submit an initial booking request, and no charge is made at submission.
  • Pay later: After review, customers may use Pay Online in My Garage or pay at service when those options are available for the booking.
  • Secure processing: Card information is handled securely by Stripe and is not stored by Cardetail1. Cardetail1 does not see or store raw card numbers, CVV, or full card data.
  • Optional saved-card authorization: If a customer intentionally saves a card in a separate payment flow, that card may only be used in accordance with the authorization shown in that flow and this policy.
  • No automatic charges: Saving a card does not itself create a charge. Applicable charges are reviewed and applied by Cardetail1 staff according to the authorization and terms below.

3. Cancellation & No-Show Policy

  • Cancellation window: Customers may cancel or request changes before 24 hours before the confirmed appointment without a cancellation fee.
  • Late cancellation: Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the confirmed appointment may be subject to a cancellation fee according to the confirmed booking amount and service conditions.
  • No-show / denied access: No-show, locked or inaccessible vehicle, denied access, unsafe location, or customer not available at the scheduled time may be subject to a no-show or access fee according to the confirmed booking amount and service conditions.
  • Fee range: Where applicable, a cancellation/no-show/access fee may apply, up to 25% of the scheduled service amount or a minimum service/travel fee, depending on the booking.
  • Fairness: If weather, emergency, or reasonable schedule conflict occurs, Cardetail1 may offer rescheduling to avoid unfair charges. All fees are reviewed by Cardetail1 staff before being applied.
  • No automatic charges: No cancellation, no-show, or access fee is charged automatically. All charges are applied manually by Cardetail1 staff and are subject to review.

4. Payment

  • Approved service payment: Cardetail1 may make Pay Online available in My Garage, send an online payment request, or use an expressly authorized saved payment method according to the booking and payment terms.
  • On-site payment: Final service payment is collected on-site by card or cash after service completion, unless booking terms state otherwise.
  • Refunds: Refunds for completed services are not issued except where Cardetail1 is at fault for a material service failure. Contact us within 24 hours of service to report an issue.

5. Weather & Rescheduling

  • Conditions requiring reschedule: Severe weather, unsafe working conditions, limited access, freezing conditions, heavy rain, extreme heat, or lack of suitable space may require rescheduling. Cardetail1 will contact you to adjust the appointment.
  • No penalty for weather reschedule: When Cardetail1 reschedules due to weather or unsafe conditions, no cancellation fee applies.

6. Pricing & Final Price

  • Starting estimates: Prices shown are starting estimates. Final pricing may change based on vehicle size, condition, pet hair, odor, stains, biohazard, excessive soil, oversized vehicles, distance, tolls, and add-ons.
  • On-site assessment: The technician may adjust the final price after assessing actual vehicle condition. Customers will be informed before proceeding with any significant price change.
  • Out-of-scope conditions: Biohazard, pest contamination, severe mold, sewage, or structurally unsafe vehicles may require a separate estimate or may be declined.

  • Offer: When Cardetail1 enables the welcome offer, eligible new customers may receive 10% off the eligible service subtotal, up to a maximum savings of $40 per qualifying booking.
  • Eligibility: One redemption per eligible customer or household. Applies to qualifying residential or specialty detailing packages. New customers only — no prior completed qualifying service.
  • Exclusions: Maintenance Detail, Fleet/commercial services, custom quotes, travel fees, tolls, congestion charges, taxes where applicable, gift-card purchases, excluded add-ons, and bookings that already include an approved discount.
  • Multiple vehicles: When multiple eligible vehicles are booked in one visit, the maximum total welcome savings remains $40 for the booking — not $40 per vehicle.
  • No promo code: The welcome offer is applied automatically when eligible. Customers cannot enter, share, or reuse an internal offer reference.
  • Cannot be combined: Unless Cardetail1 explicitly enables stacking in policy, the welcome offer cannot be combined with other promotional offers.
  • Server authority: Eligibility, discount amount, and final approved total are calculated by Cardetail1 servers. Displayed estimates may update after review.
  • Availability: Cardetail1 may enable or disable this offer at any time. Production availability is subject to Cardetail1 review.

7. Access Requirements

  • Customer responsibility: The customer must provide safe, legal access to the vehicle, enough working space around the vehicle, and permission to perform the service at the location.
  • Access fee: If the vehicle cannot be accessed at the scheduled time — locked, blocked, moved, or customer unavailable — a no-show/access fee may apply according to the cancellation policy above.

8. Personal Belongings

  • Remove personal items: Please remove personal items, valuables, and documents from the vehicle before service. Cardetail1 is not responsible for unsecured personal belongings left inside the vehicle during service.

9. Photos & Documentation

  • Service photos: Before/after photos may be taken for service documentation, quality control, dispute resolution, and portfolio use unless the customer requests otherwise prior to service.
  • Privacy: Photos are not shared publicly without consent. Cardetail1 may use anonymized service photos for training and marketing unless the customer opts out.
