Two films to compare

Carbon and ceramic are different films. The comparison below uses demo pricing for this preview. It is not a price you can book.

Carbon Window Tint

A color film that changes how dark the glass looks and can soften glare. Heat control is separate, so this preview does not give it a number.

Ceramic Window Tint

A different film from carbon. People compare it when shade and heat control should be considered apart. It is not ceramic coating, and it is not applied to paint.

What to know before choosing

Ceramic film is not ceramic coating

Ceramic window film sits on glass. Ceramic coating is a paint service. Booking one does not include the other.

Shade and heat are different

A darker look does not by itself describe heat control. This preview does not publish a heat number or a darkness level.

The final choice is specific

It depends on the glass, the product, and the rules for the state where the vehicle is registered. Front glass is not treated as allowed at every shade.
