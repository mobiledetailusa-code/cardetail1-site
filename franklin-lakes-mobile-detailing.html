How we work in Franklin Lakes

Franklin Lakes (07417) is in northwest Bergen County, inside our standard service radius with no separate travel fee. We come to the house with water, power, and our own equipment and detail the car where it is parked, usually in the driveway or in front of the garage. Starting prices are the same catalog as the homepage: Interior Detail from $200 for a sedan, Premium Full Detail from $250 (two-row SUVs from $285, three-row from $310), and Exterior Refresh & Protect from $335.

Before we confirm the day, tell us how to reach the car: a gate code, a long driveway, a detached garage, or a spot where the van can stand next to the vehicle for the whole job. If the car lives in a closed garage, we can work at the garage apron. Larger SUVs and three-row vehicles are priced by size, so pick the right vehicle class at booking and the total will match.

Winter is when most cabins need the most help: road salt on the carpets, wet floor mats, and dust on vents. Interior Detail covers vacuuming, fabric shampoo, steam on vents and panels, leather conditioning, and interior glass. Pet hair ($95) and odor treatment ($90) are optional lines you add only if you need them. On the outside, Premium Full Detail adds a hand wash, clay bar decontamination, and hand-applied premium paste wax.

Exterior Refresh & Protect is the exterior-only package for paint that looks dull or rough: clay, polish, sealant, wheels, and glass. If you want longer-term protection, ceramic coating is quoted separately on /ceramic-coating. We confirm exterior work against the weather, so a cold or wet day may move the exterior steps; interior work is less affected. Appointments run Monday–Saturday · By Appointment Only.
