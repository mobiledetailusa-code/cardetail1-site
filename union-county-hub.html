Union County

From Elizabeth to the Summit hill towns

Union County is south of our Palisades Park base, not part of the Bergen route. Elizabeth is about 24 miles. Summit is about 29, Westfield about 32, Cranford sits between them, and Plainfield is about 37. All of those are inside the 50-mile route, so a standard job has no mileage fee.

The Garden State Parkway and Route 22 put a fine grit on light paint, and commuter cars collect it through the week. Interior Detail starts at $200 for sedans. Premium Full Detail starts at $250 for sedans, $285 for SUVs, and $310 for 3-row SUVs. Exterior Detail & Paint Enhancement starts at $335.

If you are in Hoboken or Jersey City, use the Hudson County page. Montclair and Newark are on the Essex County page. Morristown and Madison are on the Morris County page. Summit is prefilled here because it is a common Union County booking. Change the ZIP if you are in another town in the county.
