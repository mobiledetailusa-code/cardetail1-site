FAQ

RV Detailing Questions

Do I need water or power at my lot?

Usually no. We bring our own water and power so storage lots and driveways without hookups are still workable when space allows.

Can you wash RV roofs?

Roof surface cleaning can be added when safe access is available. Tell us about ladders, height, and membrane type during booking.

How do you price different RV types?

Bookable packages start at the listed “From” prices and scale with exact length, condition, and access — including slide-outs and oxidation.

Do you detail fifth wheels and Class A coaches?

Yes — travel trailers, fifth wheels, Class C, and larger coaches when site access is safe for equipment.

What about black streaks and oxidation?

Exterior Wash includes streak reduction. Heavy oxidation belongs on Premium Exterior or a Paint Correction quote after assessment.

Which package should most owners book?

Premium Complete Detail is the recommended flagship — exterior finish care plus luxury interior presentation in one visit.

Ready for Premium RV Care?

Start with Premium Complete Detail, or book Exterior / Interior packages online. Correction and membership paths are confirmed by phone.
