How RV pricing works

Length, Height, Roof Access & Condition

RV pricing reflects the real size of the job—not a generic car wash rate.

Exact length

Travel trailers, fifth wheels, and motorhomes are quoted by overall length.

Height & clearance

Tall profiles and tight lots affect equipment setup and timing.

Roof & access

Safe roof access, ladders/scaffolding needs, and sealant condition are reviewed when applicable.

Oxidation & slide-outs

Chalking, streaks, and slide-out complexity can change package selection and price.

FAQ

RV Detailing Questions

Do I need water or power at my lot?

Usually no. We bring our own water and power so storage lots and driveways without hookups are still workable when space allows.

Can you wash RV roofs?

Roof washing and treatment can be scheduled when safe access is available. Tell us about ladders, height, and membrane type during booking.

How do you price different RV types?

Packages start at the listed “From” prices and scale with exact length, condition, and access—including slide-outs and oxidation.

Do you detail fifth wheels and Class A coaches?

Yes—travel trailers, fifth wheels, Class C, and larger coaches when site access is safe for equipment.

What about black streaks and oxidation?

Exterior packages include streak reduction. Heavy oxidation is best handled under Premium Exterior after assessment.
