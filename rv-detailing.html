FAQ

RV Detailing Questions

Do I need water or power at my lot?

Usually no. We bring our own water and power so storage lots and driveways without hookups are still workable when space allows.

Can you wash RV roofs?

Roof surface cleaning can be added when safe access is available. Tell us about ladders, height, and membrane type during booking.

How do you price different RV types?

Packages start at the listed “From” prices and scale with exact length, condition, and access — including slide-outs and oxidation.

Do you detail fifth wheels and Class A coaches?

Yes — travel trailers, fifth wheels, Class C, and larger coaches when site access is safe for equipment.

What is One-Step Machine Polish?

A one-step polish is designed to improve gloss and reduce light oxidation, haze, and minor surface imperfections. It is not a multi-stage restoration and will not remove every defect.

Which package should most owners book?

Premium Complete Detail is the most popular choice — exterior wash, one-step gloss improvement, protection, and a full interior detail in one visit.
