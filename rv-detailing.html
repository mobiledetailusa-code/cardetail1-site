FAQ

RV Detailing Questions

Do I need water or power at my lot?

Usually no. We bring our own water and power so storage lots and driveways without hookups are still workable when space allows.

Can you wash RV roofs?

Roof surface cleaning can be added when safe access is available. Tell us about ladders, height, and membrane type during booking.

How do you price different RV types?

Packages use per-foot rates with package minimums. In booking, select your RV type and enter exact length on the Vehicle step — your service subtotal appears before ZIP and travel fee in Info & Schedule. Condition, access, and add-ons can change the final price.

Do you detail fifth wheels and Class A coaches?

Yes — travel trailers, fifth wheels, Class B/C, Class A, Airstream, and specialty trailers when site access is safe for equipment.

What is One-Step Machine Polish?

A one-step polish is designed to improve gloss and reduce light oxidation, haze, and minor surface imperfections. It is not a multi-stage restoration and will not remove every scratch, severe oxidation, damaged decal or permanent water spot.

Which package should most owners book?

Full RV Detail is the most popular choice — complete interior cleaning plus exterior wash and protection without machine polishing. Choose Premium Complete when you want one-step exterior polish plus full interior care.

Ready to Book Your RV?

Book Full RV Detail — our most popular package — or choose Maintenance Wash, Light Interior, Interior Detail, Premium Exterior, or Premium Complete.
