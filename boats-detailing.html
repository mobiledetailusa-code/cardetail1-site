Access limitations

FAQ

Boat Detailing Questions

Do you detail boats in the water?

We can work at many marinas and docks when access and marina rules allow. Some in-water or discharge restrictions may limit certain steps or require haul-out/storage locations instead.

Do you clean underwater hulls?

No. Cardetail1 focuses on above-waterline hull, deck, cockpit, and accessible interior marine surfaces.

How is pricing calculated?

Starting prices are shown by package. Final pricing uses exact vessel length, condition, oxidation, and access.

Do you bring water and power?

Yes—our mobile setup is designed to work without relying on marina hookups whenever feasible.

Can you work at my storage lot?

Often yes, when the facility allows vendor access and there is safe staging space for equipment.

What if my boat has heavy oxidation?

Premium packages include assessment and light improvement. Severe oxidation may need a custom review before confirming results or price.
