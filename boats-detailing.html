How boat pricing works

Exact Length, Condition & Access

Boat pricing is never one-size-fits-all. Enter your ZIP in booking to unlock local length-based pricing.

Exact vessel length

Quotes scale with overall length so pricing stays transparent for smaller boats through larger cruisers.

Condition & oxidation

Heavy oxidation, chalking, and neglected surfaces usually require a heavy-cutting compound stage. We evaluate your boat's finish to apply the exact restoration level needed to bring back its original deep color and shine.

Access limitations

Tight docks, steep ramps, and limited staging space affect time and final pricing.

Water & marina rules

Some marinas restrict discharge or power. We plan around on-site rules and bring our own water when needed.

FAQ

Boat Detailing Questions

Do you detail boats in the water?

We can work at many marinas and docks when access and marina rules allow. Some in-water or discharge restrictions may limit certain steps or require haul-out/storage locations instead.

Do you clean underwater hulls?

No. Cardetail1 focuses on above-waterline hull, deck, cockpit, and accessible interior marine surfaces.

How is pricing calculated?

Starting prices are shown by package. Final pricing uses exact vessel length, condition, oxidation, and access.

Do you bring water and power?

Yes—our mobile setup is designed to work without relying on marina hookups whenever feasible.

Can you work at my storage lot?

Often yes, when the facility allows vendor access and there is safe staging space for equipment.

What if my boat has heavy oxidation?

Premium Marine focuses on heavy gel-coat oxidation removal and deep gloss revival with professional machine buffing. For severely chalked or neglected finishes, we'll review photos or an on-site look so we can tailor the right restoration plan before confirming results or price.
