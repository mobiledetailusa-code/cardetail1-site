Rockland County

Driveway detailing west of the Hudson

Rockland sits just north of the New Jersey line. From our Palisades Park base, Piermont is about 12 miles, Nyack about 15, and New City and Suffern about 20. Pearl River, Nanuet, Tappan, and Orangeburg are on the same route. We work in the driveway and bring water and power.

River Road and the Palisades parkway leave brake dust and a dull film on paint that a tunnel wash does not remove. Interior Detail starts at $200 for sedans. Premium Full Detail starts at $250 for sedans, $285 for SUVs, and $310 for 3-row SUVs. Exterior Refresh & Protect starts at $335. Those are the same prices as the homepage.
