Frequent Questions

What is the difference between a car wash and detailing?

A car wash removes loose dirt quickly. Detailing is a longer inspected process: safer wash method, wheels and jambs, interior extraction where needed, and protection that lasts beyond a few days.

What is mobile detailing?

Mobile detailing is professional car detailing at your home, office, marina, or approved location. Cardetail1 brings water, power, and equipment so you do not visit a shop or tunnel.

Is mobile detailing better than a car wash?

For paint safety, interiors, and lasting protection, yes. A typical wash is a short rinse. Mobile detailing spends hours on the vehicle and can include decontamination, extraction, and a sealant or coating.

What is the difference between a car wash and a professional detail?

A typical car wash removes loose dirt quickly. A professional detail is a longer, inspected process: safer wash method, wheels and jambs, interior extraction where needed, and protection that lasts beyond a few days. Read the full side-by-side in Detailing vs. Car Wash.

Is detailing better for paint than a tunnel wash?

Usually yes. Tunnel brushes and dirty mitts can grind grit into clear coat and leave swirl marks. A detail uses controlled contact washing, decontamination when needed, and a sealant or coating instead of a fast soap-and-rinse.

How often should I detail my car?

Most daily drivers do well with a full or interior detail every 2 to 4 months, plus lighter exterior upkeep between visits. Winter salt, pets, kids, and garage-kept cars change that cadence. See How Often to Detail.

Do you come to my home or office?

Yes. Cardetail1 is mobile from Palisades Park, NJ. We come to approved driveways, offices, marinas, and storage locations across Bergen County and nearby NJ, NY, CT, and PA by quote.

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

No. Water and power access are optional planning notes. We bring our own water and power whenever the location allows safe staging.

How do I book, and is a card required?

Submit a booking request online. No card or payment method is required to send the request, and a request does not confirm an appointment. After review you can pay online in My Garage or at service when those options are available.

How long does a mobile detail take?

Interior-only visits are often a few hours. Full details take longer. Condition, vehicle size, pet hair, and add-ons change the window. We share a realistic window when the job is reviewed.

What if it rains?

Unsafe rain, freezing conditions, or poor access can require a reschedule. When Cardetail1 moves a visit for weather, no cancellation fee applies. We will contact you to pick a new window.

Can you remove pet hair, stains, or winter salt?

Pet hair, stains, odor, and heavy salt film are common reasons people book a detail instead of a wash. Severe soil, biohazard, or mold may need a separate estimate or may be declined after review.

Do you detail boats, RVs, and motorcycles too?

Yes. Dedicated pages cover boats, RVs and trailers, and powersports. Boat work is above the waterline.
