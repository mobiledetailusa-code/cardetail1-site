Frequent Questions

What is the difference between a car wash and detailing?

A car wash is usually enough when the vehicle mainly has loose road dirt. Detailing is a longer, inspected process that can go after embedded contamination, interior soil, and protection — depending on the package. The two are not interchangeable.

Is detailing worth it if my car is not very dirty?

Sometimes. Light road film does not always need a deep detail. It is more useful when the cabin has stains, pet hair, odors, neglected plastics, or the paint feels rough after a normal wash. If a maintenance clean still leaves the car feeling used, a detail is the next step.

Can detailing remove every stain?

No. Many stains lighten or improve with extraction and targeted cleaning. Dye transfer, long-set spills, and worn fibers may not disappear completely. We will say so if a mark looks permanent after review.

Can detailing remove odors completely?

It depends on the source. Surface odor in fabric often improves with cleaning. Odor absorbed into padding, HVAC, or from smoking or pets may need deeper treatment and still may not vanish in one visit. We do not promise a scent-free cabin from photos or a basic wipe-down.

Does a basic interior detail include shampoo?

Not automatically. A basic maintenance interior is typically vacuum plus wipe-down. Shampoo, extraction, and odor work are different jobs and depend on the package or add-ons selected during booking.

How long does mobile detailing take?

Interior-only visits are often a few hours. Full details take longer. Vehicle size, soil, pet hair, and add-ons change the window. We share a realistic arrival window when the request is reviewed.

Do I need to be home for mobile detailing?

Someone needs to provide vehicle access and stay reachable. You do not have to watch the entire job. Keys or app access, a clear staging area, and a way to reach you if weather or condition questions come up are enough.

Does mobile detailing require water or electricity?

No. Water and power access are optional planning notes. Cardetail1 brings water and power whenever the location allows safe staging.
