What ceramic coating helps with

  • Makes routine washing easier.
  • Adds gloss and a smooth hydrophobic finish.
  • Helps resist road grime, salt and environmental contamination.
  • Lasts longer than traditional wax when properly maintained.

What ceramic coating does not do

  • Does not prevent rock chips or dents.
  • Does not make paint immune to scratches.
  • Does not remove existing paint defects.
  • Does not eliminate the need for proper washing.

How ceramic coating is installed

1. Inspect

We inspect the paint and identify contamination, water spots and correction needs.

2. Prepare

The exterior is washed and decontaminated so the coating can bond properly.

3. Enhance

Paint enhancement follows the booked package. Additional correction requires customer approval.

4. Apply and cure

The coating is applied panel by panel and the vehicle remains protected during the initial cure.

A suitable indoor workspace is required

Ceramic coating must be applied in a clean, dry, enclosed and properly ventilated area protected from wind, dust, rain and direct weather exposure. The vehicle must remain dry and protected for at least 24 hours after application.

We confirm the workspace before approving the appointment. If the location is not suitable, we will discuss another location or service option.

Caring for your coating

  • Keep the vehicle dry during the initial cure.
  • Avoid automatic brush washes.
  • Use a proper hand-wash method.
  • Remove bird droppings and contamination promptly.
  • Contact Cardetail1 if the coating needs an inspection.

Questions about ceramic coating

How long does ceramic coating last?

The booked package is up to 1 year or up to 3 years. Expected durability depends on preparation, use, and maintenance. It is not a fixed result for every vehicle.

Can ceramic coating stop scratches?

No. It does not make paint immune to scratches. It also does not prevent rock chips or dents, and it does not replace paint protection film.

Does my vehicle need paint correction?

Paint condition is checked before application. The package includes a one-stage gloss-enhancement polish. It does not include deep paint correction or complete defect removal. Any additional correction is quoted separately and requires your approval.

Can ceramic coating be applied outdoors?

No. It must be applied in a clean, dry, enclosed, and properly ventilated area, protected from wind, dust, rain, and direct weather. We confirm the workspace before approving the appointment.

Can you apply it in my garage?

Yes, when the garage is clean, dry, enclosed, and properly ventilated, and the vehicle can stay dry and protected during the initial cure. If the space is not suitable, we will discuss another location or service option.

When can the vehicle get wet?

Keep the vehicle dry for at least 12 hours after application, and do not wash it for 7 days. That is the cure guidance in the current ceramic catalog. The vehicle should also stay protected from weather while it cures.

How should I wash a coated vehicle?

Use a proper hand-wash method. Avoid automatic brush washes, and remove bird droppings and other contamination promptly.

Does ceramic coating prevent rock chips?

No. It does not prevent rock chips or dents. Ceramic coating does not replace paint protection film.

Is interior detailing included?

No. These packages are painted exterior only. Interior detailing is optional and is chosen separately during booking.

Are wheels and glass included?

Normal cleaning of wheels, tires, and exterior glass is included. Ceramic coating on wheels, glass, or trim is included only when that add-on is explicitly selected during booking. It is not part of the base package.
