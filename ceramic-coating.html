What it actually is

Coating protects the finish. It does not create one.

Ceramic coating is a liquid that bonds to clear coat. Done on clean, corrected paint, it sheds water and makes road film easier to wash off than a spray sealant. It does not fill rock chips, cover deep scratches, or replace paint protection film on a bumper.

FAQ

Ceramic coating questions

Is ceramic coating included in a detail package?

No. Premium Full Detail, Exterior Refresh & Protect, and Signature Restoration use sealant. Ceramic coating is a separate quote.

How is ceramic coating different from the sealant you can book online?

The polymer sealant add-on and the sealant in Exterior Refresh sit on the paint for about 3 to 6 months. Ceramic coating is a bonded layer quoted per vehicle after we see the paint.

Will coating hide scratches?

No. Coating locks in the finish underneath. Dull paint, swirls, and scratches need correction first, or they stay visible.

Do you coat the car at my house?

Yes. We come from Palisades Park to driveways and approved lots in Bergen County and nearby areas when the car can stay dry for the cure window. Extended areas are reviewed the same way as other mobile visits.

Does ceramic coating include interior detailing or paint protection film?

No. Interior work is its own package. Paint protection film, glass coating, wheel coating, and trim coating are included only when the quote lists them.
