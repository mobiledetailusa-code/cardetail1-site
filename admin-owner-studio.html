Runtime

Editing and publication controls are intentionally unavailable until services, authorization, and tests pass. With OWNER_STUDIO_ENABLED=false (default), the public website remains on legacy content.

Control-plane modules

Owner Studio is the authoritative control plane for the website's catalog, pricing and content. Only modules that are actually implemented are openable — planned modules are shown for roadmap context and are intentionally not navigable.

  • Catalog

    Available

    Packages, add-ons, vehicle classes and integer-cent pricing. Draft → revision → restore, with conflict tracking.

    Open Catalog Manager →

  • Pricing & Travel

    Planned

    Travel-distance pricing, free radius, per-mile charges and fixed zones. Contract defined; module not yet built.

    Not yet available

  • Content & Media

    Planned

    Cover images, galleries, alt text, page copy and SEO. Schema exists; editor not yet built.

    Not yet available

  • Service Areas

    Planned

    State, city and ZIP availability. Schema exists; editor not yet built.

    Not yet available

  • Publishing

    Planned

    Transactional publish of an immutable catalog release to the public site. Publish/rollback remain disabled.

    Not yet available

Delivery stages

Owner Studio never becomes the runtime authority for any domain until a catalog version is published and PUBLIC_CONTENT_SOURCE=owner-studio. Stage 6 is the gate for that cutover.
