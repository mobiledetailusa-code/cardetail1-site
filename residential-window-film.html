Three films to compare

Pick one direction for this preview. The choice is kept when the form opens. None of these cards is a price.

Solar control

A film chosen for heat and glare. This preview does not give a heat number, a brand, or a warranty.

Privacy

A darker film that can make it harder, during the day, to see into a room from outside. At night, when the lights are on inside, people outside can still see in. It is not a lock and it is not protection against intrusion.

Decorative / frosted

A frosted or patterned film that changes the look of the glass and can soften a clear view. It is not protection against intrusion.

What this preview will and will not say

Area only

Width, height, unit, and the number of panes are added up in square feet. No price is attached.

Glass type can be unknown

Single pane, double pane, tempered, laminated, or I don't know. Recording the type does not decide whether a film fits the glass.

Night privacy has a limit

Privacy film is about the view from outside. Interior lights at night can make the room visible again. That limit is not a promise of seclusion.

Measure the glass

The booking form keeps the film you picked. It asks for the number of panes, width and height with an explicit unit, a way to repeat a size, the floor and access, and the glass type. Contact and address are the fields the detailing form already uses. Nothing is sent.

Questions

How long does the work take?

A residential duration is not set. This preview does not offer a time, and it does not use the automotive tint duration.

Will the film fit my glass?

This page does not decide that. I don't know is an acceptable answer for the glass type. Compatibility stays unconfirmed.

Does privacy film hide the room at night?

No. During the day it can make the view in harder. At night, with the lights on inside, people outside can still see in.

Does the film stop someone from entering?

This page does not claim that. Solar control, privacy, and decorative film are not presented as protection against intrusion.

No warranty is offered on this page. Terms are not confirmed.
