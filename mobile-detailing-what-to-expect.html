What Is Mobile Detailing? What to Expect

Mobile detailing should feel simpler than a tunnel: we come to you. The tradeoff is space, access, and a real block of time on the calendar. Here is how a Cardetail1 visit actually runs.

1. You send a request — not a locked appointment

Online booking is a request. It does not confirm the slot by itself. We review location, vehicle condition, service type, weather, access, and availability, then send a separate confirmation. No card or payment method is required to send the request. After review, you can pay online in My Garage or at service when those options are available.

2. We come to an approved location

Most jobs are driveways, office lots, marinas, or storage sites that allow vendor access. We need safe staging for hoses, tanks, and walking around the vehicle. Tight urban parking, HOA rules, or a blocked driveway can change timing or require a different address. Tell us the constraints in the request so we do not discover them on arrival.

Water and power are optional notes, not a prerequisite

We bring our own water and power whenever the site allows safe staging. If you have a spigot or outlet, mention it — it can help planning. You do not need them to book.

3. Arrival windows are estimates

Traffic, a prior job running long, or access issues can shift the window. We communicate when that happens. Please keep the vehicle accessible, unlocked as agreed, and free of valuables you do not want moved.

4. Condition can change the plan

Photos and notes in the request help. On site, pet hair, stains, oxidation, or odor may need add-ons or a revised total. You will hear about a significant price change before we proceed. Some conditions (biohazard, heavy mold, unsafe vehicles) may be declined or quoted separately.

5. Photos protect both sides

Service photos may be taken to document pre-existing damage and finished work. That is in the booking terms. It is not a public gallery unless you agree otherwise.

6. Weather can move the day

Heavy rain, freezing conditions, extreme heat, or unsafe footing can force a reschedule. When Cardetail1 moves a visit for weather or safety, no cancellation fee applies. Late customer cancellations and no-shows follow the terms.

7. Payment after the work

Final payment is typically collected after the job (card or cash on site) unless the booking uses Pay Online in My Garage. Saving a card, when you choose that separate flow, does not by itself create a charge.

How long will you be here?

Interior-only is often a few hours. Full details take longer. Size, condition, and add-ons matter more than the package name on the card. If you need the driveway back by a hard stop, say so in the request.

Visit-day questions

What is mobile detailing?

Mobile detailing is professional car detailing at your location. Cardetail1 comes to an approved driveway, office, marina, or storage site with water, power, and equipment.

Do I need to stay with the car?

Someone with access should be reachable. You do not have to watch the entire job, but we need keys or app access and a way to reach you if weather or condition questions come up.

Can you work in an apartment lot?

Sometimes, when management allows vendors and there is safe space to stage. Note the lot rules in your request. If it is not workable, we will say so before confirming.

Where do I manage the booking after I submit?

My Garage is the customer portal for lookup, balances, and updates after the request is in the system.
