Suggested Booking Terms Summary

  • Booking request: submitting this form does not guarantee the appointment until Cardetail1 confirms by text, call, or email.
  • Time windows: arrival windows are estimates only. Traffic, weather, prior jobs, parking, tolls, and access issues may cause delays.
  • Weather: rain, storms, freezing temperatures, unsafe heat, wind, or lack of safe covered access may require rescheduling with no penalty.
  • Cancellation / rescheduling: please request changes at least 24 hours before the appointment. Rescheduling is preferred over cancellation.
  • Late cancellation: cancellations made within 24 hours, same-day cancellation, unavailable vehicle/vessel, no-show, denied access, or unsafe location may result in a cancellation fee or loss of deposit.
  • Fairness policy: if weather, emergency, or reasonable schedule conflict occurs, Cardetail1 may offer rescheduling to avoid unfair charges.
  • Final price: online totals are starting estimates. Excessive dirt, pet hair, odor, stains, mold, oxidation, black streaks, distance, tolls, parking, difficult access, or add-ons may change the final price.
  • Unsafe/out-of-scope conditions: biohazard, pest contamination, abandoned units, sewage, mold, severe odor, unsafe roofs, or damaged surfaces may require estimate or may be declined.
