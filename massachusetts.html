Massachusetts Service — FAQ

Is MA service available immediately?
No — Massachusetts service is partner-operated with limited availability. We review each request individually and confirm before your booking is finalized. You will not be charged until confirmed.
Which MA areas do you primarily serve?
We focus on Greater Boston, inner suburbs (Newton, Wellesley, Lexington, Weston, Concord), the North Shore (Marblehead, Salem), and the South Shore (Duxbury, Cohasset, Hingham). Enter your ZIP above to check availability.
Why is there a regional fee?
A +$35 Massachusetts regional fee applies to cover the travel distance and coordination from our base in Bergen County, NJ. This is shown before you confirm — no surprises.
Do you detail boats and RVs in MA?
Yes — we can detail boats at marinas and RVs at storage facilities or your driveway, subject to partner availability. Mention your vessel type when submitting your request.
Is pricing the same as NJ?
Base service pricing is the same, plus the regional fee. Final pricing is confirmed by our team before your appointment and may vary based on vehicle condition or travel specifics.
