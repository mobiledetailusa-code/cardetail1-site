Connecticut Service — FAQ

Is CT service available immediately?
No — Connecticut service is partner-operated with limited availability. We review each request individually and confirm before your booking is finalized. You will not be charged until confirmed.
Which CT areas do you serve?
We currently focus on Fairfield County (Greenwich, Stamford, Norwalk, Westport, Darien, New Canaan) and nearby areas. Enter your ZIP above to check your specific location.
Why is there a regional fee?
A +$25 Connecticut regional fee applies to cover the travel distance from our base in Bergen County, NJ. This is added to the service price and shown before you confirm.
Is pricing the same as NJ?
Base service pricing is the same, plus the regional fee. Final pricing is confirmed by our team before your appointment and may vary based on vehicle condition or travel specifics.
