Suggested Booking Terms Summary

  • Booking: Submitting this form sends a booking request. Cardetail1 reviews your service, location, access, weather, and availability before final confirmation.
  • Service photos: Cardetail1 may take before/after photos of the vehicle during service for quality improvement, training, and the documentation/security of both parties.
  • Pre-existing damage: Cardetail1 is not responsible for pre-existing damage (scratches, dents, chips, stains, mechanical or electrical issues, worn or aged components) present before service.
  • New damage from vehicle condition: due to the condition and nature in which some vehicles are found, new issues may occur during normal detailing — e.g. loose or brittle trim/clips, failing clearcoat or paint, cracked or aged plastics, worn seals or water intrusion, or peeling films. These are a result of the vehicle's existing condition and are not the responsibility of Cardetail1.
  • Time windows: arrival windows are estimates only. Traffic, weather, prior jobs, parking, tolls, and access issues may cause delays.
  • Weather: rain, storms, freezing temperatures, unsafe heat, wind, or lack of safe covered access may require rescheduling with no penalty.
  • Cancellation / rescheduling: please request changes at least 24 hours before the appointment. Rescheduling is preferred over cancellation.
  • Late cancellation: cancellations made within 24 hours, same-day cancellation, unavailable vehicle/vessel, no-show, denied access, or unsafe location may result in a fee under the booking policy.
  • Fairness policy: if weather, emergency, or reasonable schedule conflict occurs, Cardetail1 may offer rescheduling to avoid unfair charges.
  • Final price: online totals are starting estimates. Excessive dirt, pet hair, odor, stains, mold, oxidation, black streaks, distance, tolls, parking, difficult access, or add-ons may change the final price.
  • Unsafe/out-of-scope conditions: biohazard, pest contamination, abandoned units, sewage, mold, severe odor, unsafe roofs, or damaged surfaces may require estimate or may be declined.
Booking Policy Summary · Read Full Terms →
  • Booking review: Submitting a request does not guarantee an appointment. We review location, vehicle condition, service type, weather, access, and availability before confirming.
  • Card on file: No charge is made when your card is saved. Card information is handled securely by Stripe and is not stored by Cardetail1.
  • Cancellation / no-show: Cancellations less than 24 hours before the confirmed appointment, no-show, inaccessible vehicle, denied access, or unsafe location may be subject to a cancellation/no-show fee according to the confirmed booking amount and service conditions.
  • Service payment: If service is completed and you are not available for on-site payment, Cardetail1 may send an online payment request or charge an approved saved payment method according to your selected payment preference and booking policy.
  • Weather / reschedule: Severe weather, unsafe conditions, freezing temperatures, or lack of suitable space may require rescheduling. We will contact you to adjust the appointment.
  • Pricing: Prices shown are starting estimates. Final pricing may change based on vehicle size, condition, pet hair, odor, stains, excessive soil, oversized vehicles, distance, tolls, and add-ons.
  • Access: You must provide safe legal access to the vehicle and enough working space. If the vehicle cannot be accessed, a no-show/access fee may apply.
  • Personal items: Please remove personal items before service. Cardetail1 is not responsible for unsecured personal belongings left inside the vehicle.
  • Photos: Before/after photos may be taken for documentation, quality control, and dispute resolution unless you request otherwise.
  • Pre-existing condition: Failing clear coat, loose trim, cracked surfaces, previous repairs, and heavily worn materials may limit results. Cardetail1 is not responsible for pre-existing defects.
