FAQ

Truck Questions

Do pickups book here?

No. Consumer pickups book under Cars & SUVs. This page is for commercial semi-trucks (day cab and sleeper cab).

Is sleeper pricing higher than day cab?

Base package dollars are the same. Loaded sleeper living areas (fridge, microwave, cabinets) may need the Super Interior add-on.

Do you wash the trailer?

Packages cover the tractor. Full trailer body wash is quoted separately when needed.

Where do you service semis?

Most jobs are at a yard, terminal, or driveway with safe staging space for our mobile unit.

Can I book multiple tractors?

Yes — use Book Multiple Vehicles or Add Another Vehicle in checkout.
